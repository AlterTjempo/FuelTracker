## How to read this

- **Saving** = (a) mean best price over the next 6 h − best price now, or (b) minimum best price over the next 12 h − best price now, in cents per litre. **Positive = going now saved money** (waiting would have cost more). Saving (b) compares with the cheapest possible moment to wait for, so it is usually negative (the 'regret' of not waiting). Cycles with missing future data are skipped, never filled.
- Sign convention: the task text defined saving as 'now − future' but also said 'positive = saved by going now', which contradict each other; the economically correct 'future − now' is used.
- **Better than waiting** = share of decisions with saving (a) ≥ 0.
- **Score (separation)** = mean saving (a) of *excellent/good* minus mean saving (a) of *wait/avoid*, in cents. A useful indicator has a clearly positive separation.
- Dev data note: the development database is not continuous; the numbers show what the data supports, not production behaviour.
